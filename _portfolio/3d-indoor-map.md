A graduation project exploring a 3D indoor map of Gachon University with hybrid mesh–Gaussian splatting representations.

### Project focus

Connected 3D reconstruction with a browser-based visualization experience using Three.js and WebGL. The interface supports building and floor selection, room markers with timetable information, indoor routes with floor transitions, and administrator controls for markers, routes, and splat settings.

### Recognition

Received a P-Project section Excellence Award and was selected as an outstanding project at the department's project presentation day.
