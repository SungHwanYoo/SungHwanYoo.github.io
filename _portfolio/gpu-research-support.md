Contributed to Gachon University's application for the **Advanced GPU Utilization Support Program**, administered by the Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) and the National IT Industry Promotion Agency (NIPA).

### My contribution

- Prepared technical business plans, research reports, and proposals explaining the feasibility of AI research, the need for GPU resources, and expected benefits.
- Analyzed computing requirements for large-dataset training and developed GPU utilization plans.
- Structured the research and technical evidence to match the program's objectives, contributing to the application's successful selection.
